**Goal:** Publish a distinctive, accurate ZIP landing grounded in the developing product.
**Architecture:** Preserve the shared Three film and demo; shorten its narrative and add a scroll-driven real-product atlas through the existing motion scheduler. Keep assets local and declarative fallback content complete.

**Spec:** ../specs/2026-11-13-product-atlas-design.md

## Global constraints

- ZipDev is read-only and its dirty work is preserved.
- No opaque text mask, decorative connectors or colored analysis plates.
- Price, estimate and missing-value provenance must remain truthful; planned layers are not current features.
- No new runtime dependency or additional continuous render loop.
- Preserve all demo calculations, URL state and dialog accessibility.

## Review focus

- A screenshot must not imply a production/live-data service; date and development status are explicit.
- Short phone/reduced-motion/no-JS reading remains complete without a sticky-image gap.
- Fast reverse scroll or anchor navigation must not show an empty tour frame.
- Models, headings, proof and dock remain spatially separate throughout transitions.
- Cached assets after CSS or module changes must resolve to this release.
