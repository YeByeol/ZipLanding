# ZIP information UI refinement

The user's four close-up screenshots are the visual evidence: oversized price numeral and chamfered white slab; disconnected condition KPIs with gold underlines; spreadsheet stripes, oversized score cells and ambiguous dotted-unit labels; arithmetic fragments preceding the price-comparison result. Passing clipping tests did not establish acceptable information design.

This is a focused information-interface refinement using Impeccable layout/typeset. Keep the existing ZIP palette, local Pretendard, large3D camera, eight-chapter story and shared90ms clock. No new visual theme, font, library or imagery.

## One reading system

Use a compact opaque ink-green surface,14px corners and one subtle functional boundary. All six information chapters share a place/topic header, grouped data and readable explanatory notes. White and mint supply text hierarchy; purple remains meaningful for scores/weights. Remove chamfer, gold/purple decorative underlines, zebra rows, giant receipt numeral and sprawling formula.

- Price: one baseline-aligned monthly rent52–54px, two secondary facts24–28px, units next to each number.
- Conditions: three definition rows, labels left, quantities and units right. Same value scale and fixed unit alignment. Scroll emphasis moves a few pixels while all facts remain readable.
- Criteria: importance and resulting A/B/C scores live in one coherent surface, separated by an actual input/result grouping. The three-column phone importance display remains automatic.
- Comparison: retain native table semantics and all seven facts. Plain-language row labels, units on numeric cells, clear score/cost/physical grouping, compact place tokens. No spreadsheet striping or underline bars.
- Price interpretation: result first, three common-zero horizontal bars next, readable calculation summary last. Monthly equivalent cost39.17 and deposit equivalent4.17 derive from the unrounded existing monthlyUnit(A), under the existing5%annual/12months assumption. They are not actual cash rent. Management exclusion, two peers and no percentage summary remain explicit.
- Missingness: same readable header and definition layout; literal missing values and prospective source plan remain separate.

Long fan-out connector lines are removed from comparison, criteria and price chart. Place IDs and the existing actual3D highlights preserve the correspondence. One subdued real projected line remains for the single-place price/condition/missing cases; no wire may cross explanatory copy.
