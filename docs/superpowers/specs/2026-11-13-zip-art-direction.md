## Argument and composition

- Discover: the city is readable through personal priorities. Large two-line statement, crafted city, one clear start action.
- Price: three numbers describe a tenancy but not its setting. A paper-toned numerical statement; rent leads, deposit and area support.
- Conditions: the same place has slope, elevation and building age. Three aligned measures with clear units and descriptions; a wider, quieter graphic.
- Criteria: weights change what the viewer values. Dominant proportion bars followed by the resulting A/B/C scores; existing exact calculation drives the motion.
- Comparison: the viewer sees the same criteria side by side. Full semantic table, restrained labels, a clear score row and a subtly emphasized A column.
- Value: comparable costs use the same unit. Dominant 1.70, proportional horizontal bars, exact unrounded calculation, assumptions visible.
- Evidence: absent evidence stays absent. Deliberate blank-value treatment, explicit missing labels and planned sources.
- Closing: let the viewer make their own choice. The ZIP mark returns as a designed ending with one action into the working demo.
